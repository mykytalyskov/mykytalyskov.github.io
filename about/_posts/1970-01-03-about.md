### Mykyta Lyskov - artist, animation director
born 1983 in Dnipro, Ukraine

#### Education:
* 1997 - 2002 studied painting at the art college(Dnipro)  
* 2012 - graduate Kyiv cinema and television University (director of animated film)  

Mykyta Lyskov began his career in the early 2000s as a  
classical painter before turning to animation directing. He  
studied animation directing at the Karpenko-Karyi Kyiv  
National University of Theatre, Cinema and Television.  
He currently lives and works in Dnipro, Ukraine. His films  
explore contemporary Ukrainian themes, which he approaches  
through absurdity, irony, and black humor. His 2019 film  
Deep Love received numerous international awards, including  
the Grand Prix at DOK Leipzig and PÖFF Shorts.  
Lyskov also collaborates with musicians, creating animated  
visuals and accompaniment for live performances. Alongside  
narrative films, he makes non-narrative and abstract  
animation, often using computer technology only minimally.  
His film Imaginary Landscapes won the Grand Prix in  
abstraction competition at Anifilm in the Czech Republic in 2022.  
Since 2023, he has been teaching animation and  
scriptwriting at Dnipro Art College in Ukraine.
